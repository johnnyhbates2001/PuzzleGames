3A

Motion system

Every animation in the app, grouped by what it has to communicate: where you are (page transitions), what your finger just did (gameplay), and what you earned (completion). Each tile loops so timings can be judged in motion. Existing keyframes that already work are kept as-is and marked; the rest are revised or new.

4 kept 9 revised 14 new

Motion follows the finger

Anything triggered by a tap resolves in under 200ms and starts at the touch point. Nothing that blocks input lasts longer than 320ms.

One idea per animation

Placement pops. Errors shake. Progress sweeps. Rewards travel. A shape never borrows another's motion.

Celebration is earned

Confetti and unlocks only fire on a solve or an award — never on a routine tap, so the big moments stay big.

Interruptible

Every effect is decorative. Tapping through it skips it; no animation gates the next move or the next screen.

02

Page transitions

Depth read as direction: forward pushes, back reveals, peers cross-fade.

Forward push

300ms · out-quint · kept

New screen slides in from the right; the old one parallaxes 28% left and dims to 82%. Home → chapters → board all use this.

Back reveal

300ms · out-quint · kept

Exact reverse: the current screen slides off right while the parent brightens back into place from -28%. Stack, not two slides.

Peer cross-fade

180ms · linear · revised

Tab-bar moves have no hierarchy, so they fade instead of sliding. The pill under the active tab keeps its own 420ms slide, so the bar leads the content.

Sheet up

320ms in / 240ms out · revised

Hints, rules, settings, pause, fail. Scrim fades with the sheet; dismissal is faster than entry so closing feels obedient. Drag-down follows the finger 1:1.

160ms + 45ms stagger · new

The nine grid squares pop in reading order using the equipped skin's colours, wordmark rises last, then the whole thing cross-fades to Home. Total 900ms, skippable on tap.

03

Gameplay

Each game gets a placement, a mistake, and a progress animation — in its own accent.

Queens · crown drop

160ms · spring · kept

Scale 0.6 → 1 with a slight overshoot, anchored on the tapped cell. Second tap removes it with the same curve reversed.

Queens · auto-X ripple

120ms · 45ms per ring · kept

Excluded cells mark themselves outward by Chebyshev distance from the crown, so the rule is visible as it propagates rather than appearing all at once.

Conflict shake

220ms shake + 900ms tint · revised

Shared by all five games. The 3px shake is short; what lingers is the danger tint on the conflicting line, so she can read why after the motion stops. Haptic: one light impact.

200ms · 55ms stagger · new

Finishing a row, column or box lights it cell by cell from the placed digit outward. This is the reward loop mid-puzzle; without it a 9×9 offers no feedback until the very end.

Zip · checkpoint hit

260ms · out-quad · new

Reaching the next number pulses two rings out of the disc and bumps it 1.12×. The one clear "correct" beat in a game that otherwise gives no per-move feedback.

Undo · retract

180ms · in-out-quad · new

Undo plays the placement backwards rather than cutting: Zip's line withdraws, a crown or digit scales back down to 0.68 and fades. Repeated taps queue at 120ms so holding undo unwinds smoothly.

06

Reduced motion & rules

Reduced motion substitutes

Nothing is simply switched off. Transitions become a 120ms opacity fade, placements appear at full scale, staggers collapse to a single step, confetti becomes one static burst that fades, and the solve sweep becomes a whole-board tint pulse. Reveals keep their fade; only travel and overshoot are removed.

Haptics pairing

Light impact on placement and checkpoint, one sharp impact on conflict, a three-beat pattern on solve, nothing on undo or navigation. Haptics follow the animation's start, never its end, and honour the existing sound/vibration setting.

Budget

Transform, opacity and filter only — no layout-animating properties on the board. Per-cell effects cap at 81 elements and are removed on completion, so a 9×9 Sudoku never holds more than one live cascade at a time.

What was dropped

Zip's per-cell stub pop (replaced by the continuous draw), the shop's skin-flip on equip (it fought the swatch grid and read as a glitch — now a 200ms cross-fade plus the equipped ring animating in), and the generic pop on Nonogram cells during drag, which stuttered on fast fills.
