How to play

Queens

1

Place exactly one queen in every row, every column, and every colored region.

2

No two queens may touch, including diagonally.

3

Tap a cell to cycle it empty → X → queen → empty, or drag across cells to mark several X's at once.

Turn on Auto X in Settings to automatically X out cells a placed queen rules out.

This auto-opens on her first visit to each game, so it's the real first impression. A worked mini-board sits beside the title; the tip keeps the lightbulb that now means "hint" everywhere else. Copy is verbatim from rules.ts.
